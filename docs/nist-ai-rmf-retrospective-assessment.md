## 1. Scope and system context

S02 Variant 1 is a self-directed portfolio MVP. It accepts synthetic CRM contact, deal, and optional association CSV exports through a Python/FastAPI audit service. Deterministic Python rules produce a ZIP report with structured findings. An Open WebUI Workspace Tool passes report content to a configured hosted model for natural-language explanation. The service does not write to CRM records and is not approved for real or sensitive customer data.

**Intended use:** demonstrate how deterministic data-quality checks can be paired with AI-assisted explanations while preserving a clear boundary between finding generation and explanation.

**Out of scope / prohibited in this portfolio configuration:** real or sensitive customer CRM data; production or customer deployment; automated CRM updates; using AI explanations as verified facts or as a sole basis for business action.

**Documented demonstration evidence:** a synthetic run reports 202 contacts, 121 deals, and 92 medium-severity missing-owner flags under a provisional business rule. These are review flags, not confirmed errors. The project records 11 passing regression tests from a user-reported local run; this assessment did not rerun them. A separate live chat demonstration was user-reported. Evidence and limitations are detailed in E-01–E-04 below.

**AI and non-AI components:** deterministic rule execution; FastAPI interface; Open WebUI adapter/orchestration; hosted generative model for explanation. The recorded demonstration names GPT-4.1-mini, but the assessment does not establish a fixed deployed model version or configuration.

**Affected parties / benefits and harms:** no real people or customer organizations are included in the synthetic scenario. In hypothetical organizational use, CRM users could benefit from reviewable findings; customers, employees, or sales teams could be affected indirectly if inaccurate or misunderstood findings influence reporting or decisions. No real-world impact study is available.

**Human oversight:** the service does not change CRM data. The documentation expects users to review results, but a formal human approval workflow is not implemented or evaluated. The model’s explanation is not independently verified against the deterministic output.

**Assessment boundary and uncertainty:** this record assesses Variant 1 only. It does not assess Variant 2, the user's organization, a real CRM deployment, the hosting provider’s controls, legal applicability, or the effectiveness of controls in operation.

## 6. Summary and bounded decision

The documented MVP demonstrates a meaningful separation: Python determines the audit findings, while a hosted model explains supplied report content. The repository documents synthetic-only use, limited data coverage, a provisional missing-owner rule, and the absence of an implemented sensitivity/approval gate and independent explanation-faithfulness verification.

**Portfolio decision:** retain S02 Variant 1 as a synthetic-data portfolio demonstration only. The current evidence does not support real customer data, production deployment, or reliance on AI explanations without human verification. Any scope expansion should first address data-flow/security review, the planned SEC-001 gate, human review expectations, and explanation evaluation.

This is a portfolio-level decision by the project owner, not an organizational risk acceptance or deployment decision. No residual risk is accepted on behalf of a company.
